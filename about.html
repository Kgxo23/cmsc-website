About CMSC

Our Story

Cizrein Marine Services Co. (CMSC) is a BIR and SEC registered partnership company, conceptualized in July 2019 by partners Franciz John Cabial and Reina Loraine Inducil. Born from their rich experience as employees of a family-owned tanker vessel and marine service company, CMSC was established as a natural extension of their expertise serving major oil companies including Petron Corporation and San Miguel Corporation.

Franciz John supervised the construction and repairs of tanker vessels and tugboats, handling daily vessel operations, fuel consumption analysis, and monitored vessel maintenance including engine repairs and PMS. Reina Loraine took charge of billing, accounting, purchasing and operations, managed contracts with charterers (oil companies), sought potential business ventures, and maintained all trading documents and licenses from MARINA, NTC, OTS, and other government agencies.

Inspired by their family business success, they merged Franciz John's field expertise in ship construction and repair with Reina's mastery of internal and external marine office work. CMSC now teams up with shipyards in Navotas and accredited Naval Architects, ensuring their people are fully backed and qualified to handle and monitor ship construction and repairs following the highest existing standards.

Our Mission

CMSC is committed to providing high-quality, world-class services that maximize customer satisfaction following international standards. We aim to create quality, innovative, and sustainable products and fabrications using state-of-the-art, safe, and seaworthy equipment that preserves life and protects the environment. We strive to raise our standards to produce superior maritime vessels that meet local and international legal standards. We invest in our people through up-to-date training and development programs, and we preserve strong alliances with partners, suppliers, contractors, clients, and customers.

Our Vision

We envision CMSC to be the leading marine services company in the Philippines and to be recognized globally as an essential contributor to the success of various industries worldwide.
